# AI tools for small business, as of September 9, 2026

**Purpose:** one fact sheet for anyone teaching an AI class to small-business owners: which tools, what they cost, what to call them.
**Researched by:** the Maryland SBDC AI Co-Working team, 2026-09-09, from vendor pricing pages plus two or three independent write-ups per vendor (sources at the bottom).
**Shelf life:** short. Model names change every few months; prices about once a year. Re-verify the four model tables the week of any class delivery.

## Start here — the four tools a small-business owner will actually meet

| Tool | Free tier gets you | Paid tier most owners would buy | Current top model name (say this) |
|------|--------------------|---------------------------------|-----------------------------------|
| **Claude** (Anthropic) | Chat on web/phone/desktop, web search, memory, file creation, code execution; Sonnet and Haiku models | **Pro, $20/mo** ($17 annual): Opus model, Projects, Research, Claude Code, Cowork, Microsoft 365 integration; Max from $100/mo | Claude Opus 5 (Fable 5.1 is the newest tier above it) |

| **Gemini** (Google) | Gemini 3.6 Flash, some 3.1 Pro, image generation, Deep Research, Live voice, Canvas, Gems, Gemini Notebook, 15 GB | **Google AI Pro, $19.99/mo**: Gemini 3.1 Pro, Veo video, Flow, Gemini in Gmail/Docs, 5 TB; AI Plus $4.99/mo; AI Ultra $99.99–199.99/mo | Gemini 3.1 Pro (3.6 Flash for speed; "Gemini Omni" is the multimodal model on paid tiers) |
| **Microsoft Copilot** | Copilot Chat is included free with a Microsoft 365 business subscription; consumer Copilot app is free | **Microsoft 365 Copilot Business, $18/user/mo** annual ($25.20 monthly), for companies up to 300 people — Copilot inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook; Researcher and Analyst agents; Copilot Studio | Say "Microsoft 365 Copilot"; Microsoft does not market a model name |

Plain-language framing for class: **the free tiers are enough to learn on and to do light personal work; anyone using AI for the business every day should pay for one tool — about $20 a month — and pick the one that lives where their documents already live** (Google Workspace → Gemini; Microsoft 365 → Copilot or Claude; otherwise Claude or ChatGPT by preference).

## Claude — detail

- **Plans:** Free $0; Pro $20/mo ($17/mo annual); Max from $100/mo (5× or 20× Pro usage, early access to new features); Team $25/seat standard or $125/seat premium ($20/$100 annual); Enterprise custom.
- **Models (Sept 2026):** Claude Fable 5.1 (newest, highest tier), Claude Opus 5, Claude Sonnet 5, Claude Haiku 4.5. Free plan has Sonnet and Haiku; Pro and up add Opus. Fable availability varies by plan — check in-app before promising it in a demo.
- **Features an owner will meet:** Projects (a folder of files + instructions the chat remembers), Skills (reusable how-to instructions Claude applies automatically), Connectors (Gmail, Google Drive, Calendar, Slack, and others), memory, web search, Research (long multi-source reports), file creation (Word/Excel/PowerPoint/PDF), Claude in Chrome (browses for you), Claude for Microsoft 365, Claude Code (developer tool — mention, don't demo), Claude Cowork (works in your folders on your computer, runs scheduled tasks), Claude Design (builds decks and pages — this project's own build tool).
- **Demo notes:** Projects and file uploads are the strongest live demos. Research reports take minutes — pre-run them.

## ChatGPT — detail

- **Plans:** Free $0; Go $8/mo; Plus $20/mo; Pro $100 or $200/mo; Business $25–125/seat/mo; Enterprise custom.
- **Models (Sept 2026):** GPT-6 Astra (Plus and up; "GPT-6 Pro" reasoning on Pro), GPT-5.6 family (Luna on Free/Go, Sol on Plus, Sol Pro on Pro, Terra inside Codex/Work), GPT-5 Thinking Mini on all tiers. These sub-names appeared in mid-2026; in class say "GPT-6" and "GPT-5.6" and skip the codenames.
- **Retired in 2026:** GPT-4.5 (June 26), GPT-5.2 (June 12), GPT-5.1 (March 11), o3 (August 26), DALL·E 3 (May 12 — replaced by ChatGPT Images, now version 2.5 as of Sept 8).
- **Features an owner will meet:** projects, custom GPTs, memory, deep research, scheduled tasks (now with webhooks), voice with video, image generation (ChatGPT Images), Codex (coding agent), ChatGPT Work desktop agent, "plugins" (2026 meaning: app connections like Gmail, Slack, Zendesk, OneNote — not the 2023 plugin store), ChatGPT Sites on Plus.
- **Demo notes:** Free tier image generation is capped at a few per day — demo images from a paid account. Ads may appear on Go.

## Gemini — detail

- **Plans:** Free $0; Google AI Plus $4.99/mo; Google AI Pro $19.99/mo; Google AI Ultra from $99.99/mo ($199.99 top tier).
- **Models (Sept 2026):** Gemini 3.1 Pro (paid), Gemini 3.6 Flash (free default), Gemini Omni (multimodal, paid tiers), Deep Think (Ultra). Say "Gemini 3" if a version number is needed.
- **Features an owner will meet:** Deep Research (free), Gemini Live (voice), Canvas, Gems (Google's custom-assistant equivalent of GPTs), Gemini in Gmail/Docs/Sheets/Vids (paid), Gemini in Chrome, Nano Banana (image generation and editing), Veo 3.1 (video, paid), Flow (creative studio), Daily Brief, Jules and Antigravity (developer tools — mention only), Gemini Spark (24/7 agent, Ultra only, U.S.).
- **Gemini Notebook (formerly NotebookLM — renamed July 16, 2026; old links redirect):** free tier gives 100 notebooks, 50 sources each, 50 chats/day, Audio and Video Overviews, mind maps, flashcards, reports. The best single tool for "put all my market research in one place." Paid tiers only raise limits; the one paid-only feature is Cinematic Video Overviews.
- **Demo notes:** Audio Overviews take a few minutes — pre-generate. Free Deep Research is the cheapest impressive demo in the room.

## Microsoft Copilot — detail

- **Plans (business):** Copilot Chat — included at no extra cost with eligible Microsoft 365 business plans (web chat, file upload, Copilot Pages). Microsoft 365 Copilot Business $18/user/mo annual ($25.20 monthly), up to 300 users. Bundles: Business Standard with Copilot $23.50/user/mo; Business Premium with Copilot $32/user/mo.
- **What the paid tier adds:** Copilot inside Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook, Teams (draft from bullets, summarize threads, ask questions of a spreadsheet, meeting recaps); Researcher, Analyst, and Facilitator agents; Copilot Studio for building custom agents; Agent Store; "LLM model choice."
- **Consumer:** the free Copilot app exists; treat the consumer paid tier as "check before you say it" — it has been repackaged more than once.
- **Demo notes:** Only demo Copilot from a licensed Microsoft 365 account; the free Copilot Chat looks different from the in-app experience and confuses rooms.

## Other tools worth naming (one line each)

- **Perplexity** — search-first assistant; free tier plus Pro about $20/mo; Comet is its browser. Good for cited answers; not a first recommendation for a small-business owner over the big four.
- **Canva** — has AI image, text, and design features most owners already use; good for the "you already have AI" moment.
- **Midjourney** — still the quality leader for illustrative images; paid only; overkill for most owners.
- **Adobe Firefly** — inside Photoshop/Illustrator/Express; commercially safer training data.
- **Gamma** — AI slide decks; fine to mention, don't demo.
- **Otter / Zoom AI Companion / Google Meet notes** — meeting transcripts; the on-ramp for "turn a meeting into an action list."
- **QuickBooks / Square / Shopify AI features** — mention that the tools they already pay for are adding AI; check settings before buying anything new.

## Plain-language definitions (use these words in every class)

- **Model** — the AI "brain" behind a chat. New ones ship every few months; the name on the button changes, the way you use it doesn't.
- **Chat** — one conversation. It forgets when it ends unless memory or a project is on.
- **Memory** — the tool remembers facts about you across chats. Check what it saved; delete what you don't want.
- **Project** (Claude, ChatGPT) / **Gem** (Gemini) / **custom GPT** (ChatGPT) — a saved setup: your files, your instructions, your tone, ready every time. The single biggest upgrade for a business owner.
- **Skill** (Claude) — a written how-to the AI follows automatically when the task matches — "here's how we write a quote." Think "SOP the AI reads."
- **Connector / plugin / integration** — permission for the AI to read and act in another app (Gmail, Drive, Calendar, Slack, QuickBooks).
- **Agent** — an AI that takes several steps on its own to finish a job: searches, opens files, writes, checks its work, comes back with a result. Deep research is the agent most owners meet first.
- **Deep research** (all four have one) — you ask a big question, it reads dozens of sources for 5–20 minutes, and returns a cited report.
- **Scheduled task** — an agent that runs on a schedule: "every Monday, summarize last week's reviews."
- **Cowork-style workflow** (Claude Cowork; ChatGPT Work; Copilot agents) — the AI works inside your folders and apps on your computer, not just in a chat box: read these files, make that spreadsheet, draft those emails.
- **Prompt** — what you type. Good prompt = task + context + format + an example.
- **Hallucination** — a confident wrong answer. Verify anything with a number, a law, or a name in it.
- **Token / usage limit** — the meter. Free plans hit it fast; paid plans mostly don't.

## Demo-safe tasks (work reliably live, on a paid account, in under three minutes)

1. Press release or success story from six bullet points.
2. Customer avatar: "Interview me, one question at a time, then write my ideal customer profile."
3. NAICS code plus three adjacent codes from a one-sentence business description (verify against census.gov/naics — 2022 codes).
4. Competitor snapshot from three website URLs (web search on).
5. Seven-day social media calendar for one business, one audience.
6. Rewrite an email in three tones.
7. Turn pasted meeting notes into next steps with owners and dates.
8. Upload a CSV of sales and ask three questions of it.
9. Draft a single-member LLC bank resolution — with the "verify with a professional" line on screen.
10. Fill three Business Model Canvas blocks from a one-paragraph description.
11. Turn a canvas assumption into a testable hypothesis and five neutral interview questions.
12. Generate a flyer image in Nano Banana or ChatGPT Images (paid account; expect 20–40 seconds).
13. Voice mode: ask a question out loud on a phone.

**Pre-run, don't run live:** deep research reports (5–20 min), Gemini Notebook Audio Overviews, anything on a free tier during a class (rate limits), anything using a real client's data.

**Fallback rule (quality checklist):** every live demo has a screenshot or saved output in the run-of-show. Wi-Fi fails; so do rate limits.
