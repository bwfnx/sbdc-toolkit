# Neoserra Milestone Taxonomy

The milestone types recorded in Neoserra, the Maryland SBDC's CRM. When a transcript
describes a completed accomplishment, match it to the closest entry below and use that
entry's exact wording — Neoserra reporting depends on the names matching, so a paraphrase
like "got certified as a minority business" does not register where "MBE Certified" does.

The source instruction says milestones "include, but are not limited to" this list, so an
accomplishment with no match here is still worth recording. Name it plainly and flag that
it fell outside the taxonomy rather than forcing it into a near-miss category.

## Matching notes

Several entries are near neighbours and get confused. A few distinctions worth holding:

- **Certification obtained vs. self-certified.** "SDB Self-certified" is a self-attestation;
  "8(A) Certification Obtained", "EDWOSB Certification Obtained", and "WOSB Certification
  Obtained" are granted by an agency. Do not record one as the other.
- **Business Established vs. Business Start Impact vs. Bought Business.** Established is a new
  entity formed; Start Impact is the SBA-reportable start of operations; Bought Business is an
  acquisition of something already running.
- **Change in Full-Time Staff and Change in Part-Time Staff are separate entries.** A transcript
  saying "we hired two people" needs the full-time/part-time split before it can be recorded.
- **Change in Sales vs. Change in Profits.** Revenue and margin are different milestones; a
  client saying "we did better this quarter" has not said which.

Where the transcript is ambiguous on one of these, record what was actually said and note the
ambiguity rather than picking a side — an incorrectly categorised milestone is harder to catch
later than a flagged one.
