Quick tips

  • Full-screen: open a slide and press F11 (Windows) or ⌃⌘F (Mac).
  • Timer (Starting Soon & Be Right Back): Space starts/pauses, R restarts; controls auto-hide and won't appear on stream.
  • OBS: add each slide as a Browser source at 1920×1080 — it scales itself to fit.
  • Edit text: host, day/time, and handle live near the top of each file.
