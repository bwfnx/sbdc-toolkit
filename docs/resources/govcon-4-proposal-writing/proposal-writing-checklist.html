Proposal-Writing Checklist

Winning That Government Contracting Award · Session 4 · Keep this next to your keyboard
The formula for every key claim: feature → quantified benefit → proof → the customer by name.

Before you write

  • Compliance matrix built: every "shall" has a row
  • Each row mapped to its Section L location and Section M factor
  • 3 or 4 win themes chosen, from your capture plan
  • Discriminators you can prove, not just claim
  • Know the evaluation basis: best value or LPTA

Compliant

  • Every requirement answered, in the RFP's order
  • Page limits, font, margins exactly as Section L says
  • Every attachment and form filled and returned
  • Amendments acknowledged
  • Due date, time and time zone on your calendar twice

Correct

  • Staffing and schedule actually work
  • Price meets the wage determination, if there is one
  • Numbers match across volumes
  • Past performance is recent and relevant
  • No claim you can't back up

Compelling

  • Every feature passes the "so what?" test
  • Customer's name about 2:1 over yours
  • Their words: if they say "plan," you say plan
  • Key point first in every paragraph
  • Original graphics, numbered, with a theme in each caption

We are pleased to submitStart with their goal

Before you submit

  • Two or three outside readers reviewed it (red team)
  • Checked against the compliance matrix, row by row
  • One last read of Section L
  • Submitted early, with a confirmation saved
